\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Earnings calls and volatility.}
\citet{qin2019what} introduced the task and the EarningsCall corpus, predicting
volatility over 3 to 30 days from the transcript and the audio. HTML
\citep{yang2020html} added a sentence-level transformer with a multi-task loss,
MAEC \citep{li2020maec} a larger corpus with sentence-aligned audio features,
VolTAGE \citep{sawhney2020voltage} and \citet{sawhney2020mm} graph and multimodal
fusion, DialogueGAT \citep{sang2022dialoguegat} a dialogue graph over the Q\&A,
and KeFVP \citep{niu2023kefvp} knowledge-enhanced features. ECC Analyzer
\citep{cao2024ecc} and Sound of Risk \citep{soundofrisk2025} are recent positive
results and come without code. Most EC and MAEC results in this line are scored
on the label and split files that VolTAGE and KeFVP redistribute, which
\S\ref{sec:substrate} audits. Three newer corpora matter here. FinCall-Surprise
\citep{fincall2025} ships raw audio and no identities. Earnings25
\citep{jiang2026earnings25} postdates every model's training data. MM-FinEval
\citep{mmfineval2026}, which appeared after this study was complete, adds slides
and twelve task labels over 2{,}045 calls with audio, but its release has no
ticker or date fields and none of the checks below. The identity recovery used
here applies to it.

\paragraph{Identity, memorization and lookahead.}
\citet{scss2024} showed, on a corpus of their own and on EC and MAEC, that
transcript representations mostly tell the model which company is speaking,
that past volatility matches or beats every transcript model, and that the
shipped labels ignore release timing. Language models recover the company even
from transcripts with the names removed \citep{memorization2025}. Lookahead bias
in language models now has diagnostics and cutoff-bounded models of its own
\citep{lookahead2025,datedgpt2026,lookaheadbench2026}, and \citet{lookahead2025}
include an earnings-call forecasting task. The post-cutoff test in
\S\ref{sec:results} complements that work by scoring frozen volatility
pipelines on a later cohort.

\paragraph{Baselines, timing and bias.}
Persistence, GARCH and HAR-RV are hard to beat
\citep{corsi2009har,christensen2023mlvol} and are often missing from
the comparisons in this line \citep[Table~\ref{tab:faithful}]{scss2024}, so
every table here is anchored on them. \citet{fastnumbers2026} anchor the earnings timeline on the press release and time its first tradable moment from news-wire stamps to predict returns. We take the release time from the EDGAR acceptance stamp of the Item~2.02 8-K to place day~0 for volatility, which no label set in this line had done. Financial-audio studies report demographic imbalance and speech-emotion models
carry social biases \citep{ama2024,docfin2022,emobias2024}, so we report a
gender diagnostic.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
Among the score comparisons in the reproduction studies they reviewed,
\citet{belz2021repro} found about 14\% exact matches, and in financial NLP
discontinued data APIs and dead repository links make matters worse
\citep{finnlp2025survey}. The reproduction study here records, per paper, what
ran as released and what had to be repaired.
