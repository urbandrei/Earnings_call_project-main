\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Earnings calls and volatility.}
\citet{qin2019what} introduced the task and the EarningsCall corpus, predicting
volatility over 3 to 30 days from the transcript and the audio. HTML
\citep{yang2020html} added a sentence-level transformer with a multi-task loss,
MAEC \citep{li2020maec} a larger corpus with sentence-aligned audio features,
VolTAGE \citep{sawhney2020voltage} and \citet{sawhney2020mm} graph and multimodal
fusion, DialogueGAT \citep{sang2022dialoguegat} a dialogue graph over the Q\&A,
and KeFVP \citep{niu2023kefvp} knowledge-enhanced features. ECC Analyzer
\citep{cao2024ecc} and Sound of Risk \citep{soundofrisk2025} are the strongest
recent results and come without code. All of these are scored on the label and
split files that VolTAGE and KeFVP redistribute, which \S\ref{sec:substrate}
audits. Three newer corpora matter here. FinCall-Surprise \citep{fincall2025} is the one
open corpus with raw audio and ships no identities. Earnings25
\citep{jiang2026earnings25} postdates every model's training data. MM-FinEval
\citep{mmfineval2026}, which appeared after this study was complete, adds tickers
and dates to a larger corpus but none of the checks below, and the checks transfer
to it unchanged.

\paragraph{Identity, memorisation and lookahead.}
\citet{scss2024} showed on their own corpus that transcript representations
mostly tell the model which company is speaking, and that the company's past
volatility beats every transcript model. Language models recover the company even
from transcripts with the names removed \citep{memorization2025}. Lookahead bias
in language models now has diagnostics and cutoff-bounded models of its own
\citep{lookahead2025,datedgpt2026,lookaheadbench2026}, but no earnings-call
pipeline had been scored on calls after its training cutoff. The post-cutoff test
in \S\ref{sec:results} does that.

\paragraph{Baselines, timing and bias.}
Persistence, GARCH and HAR-RV \citep{corsi2009har} are hard to beat and often
missing from published comparisons \citep{gukellyxiu2020}, so every table here is
anchored on them. \citet{fastnumbers2026} time earnings releases from 8-K filings
to predict returns. We use the same filings to place day~0, which no volatility
label set had done. Audio features encode speaker gender
\citep{ama2024,emobias2024}, so we report the gender confound.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
A systematic review found that only 14\% of reported NLP scores were reproduced
exactly \citep{belz2021repro}, and in financial NLP discontinued data APIs and
dead repository links make matters worse \citep{finnlp2025survey}. The
reproduction study here records, per paper, what ran as released and what had to
be repaired.
