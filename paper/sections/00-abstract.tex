Since 2019, researchers have predicted post-earnings-call stock volatility from
what executives say and how they sound, and many report beating forecasts from
past price swings. We audit the EC/MAEC label and split files that several
studies reuse from earlier work. They leave no gap between training and test
although targets run 30 days ahead, 44--80\% of test calls come from companies
seen in training, and day zero sits on the call date although most companies
report before the open. We release an evaluation protocol and audit toolkit that
make these assumptions testable. The protocol recovers company names and dates
for an audio corpus that ships without them, takes release times from SEC
filings, provides targets under both horizon definitions and a forward-looking
variant, and splits that separate training and test in time and in company. The
toolkit reports company-level diagnostics on any prediction file. Under it, the
open text and audio models we evaluate show no consistent improvement over past
volatility alone by a margin fixed in advance, and their embeddings mostly
identify the company. Five published models rerun under the same checks agree,
and HAR-RV stays ahead of every content model after the training cutoff.
