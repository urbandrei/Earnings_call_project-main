Since 2019, researchers have predicted post-earnings-call stock volatility from
what executives say and how they sound, often reporting gains over
past-volatility forecasts. We audit the EC/MAEC label and split files reused
across studies. They leave no gap between training and test although targets run
30 days ahead, 44--80\% of test calls come from companies seen in training, and
day zero sits on the call date although most companies report before the open.
We release a protocol and audit toolkit that make these assumptions testable.
The protocol recovers company names and dates for an audio corpus that ships
without them, takes release times from SEC filings, and provides targets under
both horizon definitions and splits disjoint in time and in company. The toolkit runs company-level diagnostics on any prediction file.
Under this protocol, open text and audio models show no consistent
improvement over past volatility by a margin fixed in advance, and
their embeddings mostly identify the company. Five published models rerun under
the same checks show the same pattern. Holding companies out raises the text
models' error by 12--82\% but not the price-only models', and HAR-RV stays
ahead of every content model after the cutoff.
