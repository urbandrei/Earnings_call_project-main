\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Ethical Considerations}

\textbf{Data and licensing.} We use openly licensed corpora and
open-weight models. FinCall-Surprise is released under Apache-2.0,
which its authors state covers the transcripts, audio and slides
\citep[Appendix~A]{fincall2025}, and which they describe as collected
from corporate websites and public reference platforms. We rely on that
license for research use, redistribute none of the raw audio or
transcripts, and release only identity and timing tables, targets,
splits and pooled per-call features, each carrying the Apache-2.0
notice and attribution. The transcripts name the companies in the text,
so the identity table records public facts about public companies
rather than restoring a withheld field, and we release no speaker-level
audio or names. MAEC is CC-BY-SA-4.0 and its derived archives are
released under the same license. Earnings25 releases its transcripts
and metadata under CC-BY-4.0. Its audio is redistributed subject to the
original providers' terms, and we release only pooled per-call features
from it. Raw audio or transcripts from sources with unclear terms (EC,
DEC, the reproduced repositories) are never redistributed. Release
times come from EDGAR, which is public-domain US government data. Daily prices
come from Yahoo Finance through the yfinance package, whose terms do not permit
redistribution, so we release derived volatility targets and never the price
series.
\textbf{Gender-related bias.} Prior financial-audio studies have reported
gender-related prediction biases \citep{ama2024,docfin2022}, and speech-emotion
models carry social biases \citep{emobias2024}. Motivated by these findings, we
report an exploratory comparison of prediction errors using a dominant-speaker
pitch proxy. This diagnostic does not establish the gender composition of our
corpora, and it is not a full assessment of gender-related fairness. \textbf{Intended use.} This is a
research tool, not investment advice. Predictions are noisy and
regime-sensitive and should not drive automated trading.
\textbf{Reproducibility.} Given the documented reproducibility problems in
financial NLP \citep{finnlp2025survey}, the environment is pinned, every data file is
checksummed, every result CSV regenerates byte for byte from its command and run
manifest, and the curated tables copy cells from those CSVs.
