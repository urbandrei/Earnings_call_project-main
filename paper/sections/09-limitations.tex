\section*{Limitations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Limitations}

\textbf{One post-cutoff quarter.} The lookahead test rests on 475 calls from a
single calm quarter and a different universe from the training corpus, so it bounds
contamination in the ordering of models, not in their absolute scores.
\textbf{Reproductions run on their own data}, not on \ecvol-bench, to keep ``the
model fails'' separable from ``the model does not transfer''; two carry known costs
(KeFVP's released EC embeddings are unavailable upstream; DialogueGAT's corpus was
never released and our port stops at its protocol). \textbf{MLP heads are
seed-unstable} (across-seed \Rtwo{} std up to 6.8 on level-$v$, 1.1 on $\Delta v$), which is why the grid uses
deterministic canonicals; MLP cells in the appendix are seed means with that caveat.
\textbf{Short-horizon \Rtwo{} inflation.} The persistence denominator is noisiest at
$\tau{=}3$ (MSE $\sim$2.8$\times$ that at $\tau{=}7$), so short-horizon gains are
easier to show. \textbf{Timestamps.} 10.7\% of FinCall and 31.4\% of MAEC calls have
no Item~2.02 filing in the window and keep a flagged after-hours fallback; intraday
releases are treated as after-hours; results under the call-date anchor of prior
label sets are not interchangeable with ours and are shipped as a variant.
\textbf{Regime sensitivity.} FinCall-Surprise spans COVID; the temporal $\tau{=}30$
failure shows long-horizon level forecasts are regime-sensitive, which is why we
emphasise $\Delta v$ and per-year breakdowns. \textbf{MAEC coverage.} Its 78.6\%
price-join rate (2015--2018 delistings) makes it a secondary corpus, and it ships no
raw audio. \textbf{Source audio.} Every FinCall file is a 40\,kbps MP3 transcode and
Earnings25 is 64\,kbps or lower, so no result here is established on lossless audio.
\textbf{No diarisation}; audio is pooled per call, and the gender analysis uses a
dominant-speaker F0 proxy. \textbf{Identity reconstruction is imperfect}: 7.1\% of
FinCall calls remain unresolved and are excluded with a reason code.
