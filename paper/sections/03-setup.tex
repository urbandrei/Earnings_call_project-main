\section{Models, Protocol and Controls}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Ladder.}
\textbf{Stage 0}: persistence ($\hat v=v_{\mathrm{pre}}$), a train-mean intercept,
EWMA ($\lambda{=}0.94$), HAR-RV \citep{corsi2009har} and GARCH(1,1). \textbf{Stage
1}: LightGBM over past-volatility features, transcript length, Q\&A turn count and
a \textbf{ticker fixed effect}, the in-house identity baseline. \textbf{Stage 2}:
transcripts segmented into prepared remarks, Q\&A and speaker turns; BGE-M3
\citep{bge2024} section-pooled embeddings (1024-d), FinBERT \citep{finbert}
sentiment aggregates and surface statistics. \textbf{Stage 3}: audio resampled to
16\,kHz mono; eGeMAPSv02 functionals \citep{opensmile}, WavLM-Large \citep{wavlm}
and emotion2vec+ \citep{emotion2vec} embeddings over 30\,s windows, mean-pooled per
call. \textbf{Stage 4}: gated fusion and a
late-fusion stack (meta-ridge over the text, audio and Stage-1 predictions, fit on
validation). From Stage~2 upward every head is reported with and without a
past-volatility block ($v_{\mathrm{pre}}$ at all horizons plus HAR-RV features);
heads are ridge ($\alpha$ selected on validation) and a shallow MLP (5 seeds). The
MLP heads are seed-unstable (across-seed \Rtwo{} std up to 6.8), so the grid uses
one pre-registered deterministic canonical per stage; MLP cells are in
Appendix~\ref{app:complete}.

\paragraph{Metrics and tests.}
We report MSE on log-RV and, as the headline, \Rtwo{} vs.\ persistence
\citep{gukellyxiu2020}, $1-\sum(y-\hat y)^2/\sum(y-\hat y_{\mathrm{persist}})^2$.
Because $\Delta v$ has a positive mean on the measured anchor, persistence
($\Delta v{=}0$) is a weak floor there, so every $\Delta v$ claim is also stated
against the train-mean intercept.
Diebold--Mariano tests \citep{dm1995} with the HLN correction compare per-call
squared errors against persistence and the preceding stage; confirmatory families
(Stage-$k$ vs.\ Stage-1 on $\Delta v$ across horizons; Stage~4 vs.\ best unimodal;
published vs.\ controlled split per reproduced model) are Holm-corrected. Per-year,
COVID-exclusion, section and gender analyses are exploratory.

\paragraph{Identity-control suite.}
\label{sec:controls}
(1)~A \emph{ticker-only} model predicts each company's training mean. (2)~A
\emph{same-company shuffle} gives each test call the transcript or audio of another
call of the same company; a \emph{global shuffle} gives it any other call. A model
unchanged under the former reads identity; one unchanged under the latter reads
nothing. (3)~A linear \emph{identity probe} predicts the ticker from frozen call
embeddings. (4)~\tickerdisjoint{} results accompany temporal ones everywhere.
\texttt{ecvol audit predictions} applies the prediction-space forms of these
controls, plus split integrity, seen-vs-unseen companies, the between-company share
of prediction variance and company-clustered significance, to any file of
(call, ticker, date, split, horizon, truth, prediction) rows, so a published result
can be audited from the one artefact its authors already have.

\paragraph{Reproductions and post-cutoff tests.}
Of seventeen papers in the lineage, one releases code that runs as released
(Same-Company-Same-Signal), seven ship code needing repairs or unshipped inputs,
and nine ship nothing usable (Table~\ref{tab:code-audit}). We re-run the five with
usable code (HTML, KeFVP, Same-Company-Same-Signal, \citealp{sawhney2020mm},
DialogueGAT) \emph{on their own data}, with the authors' code where it can be made
to start and a line-by-line port where it cannot (every patch in a released ledger),
first against the published number, then under an embargoed and a
\tickerdisjoint{} split of the same data. Earnings25 supports two further tests: the
frozen Stage-3 ladder by bitrate stratum, and the entire FinCall-trained pipeline
scored, without retraining, on calls held after every encoder's knowledge cutoff.
