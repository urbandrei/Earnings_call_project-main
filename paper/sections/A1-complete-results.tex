\subsection{Reproduction study}
\label{app:repro}
Table~\ref{tab:repro} is the first entry of the study: the HTML head over our frozen
BGE-M3 chunk sequences on EC under the published split, an embargoed split and a
\tickerdisjoint{} split with identical labels. Table~\ref{tab:faithful} re-runs the
five papers with usable code on their own data with the authors' code wherever it
runs and a line-by-line port where it cannot; the released fidelity ledger records
every patch and substitution. Table~\ref{tab:repro-controls} gives the absolute
values behind Table~\ref{tab:repro-main}, and Table~\ref{tab:code-audit} the
code-availability audit of the seventeen papers.

\textbf{Same-Company-Same-Signal} reproduces: its TSMixer baseline matches the 76
published cells to a median $10^{-5}$ from the shipped data alone, its TMLP text
model on the authors' OpenAI embeddings matches per-window means within 0.01 and
recovers the paper's finding that embeddings shuffled within a company do as well as
the real ones, and its training-free baselines on EC and MAEC reproduce all 48
published cells at the notebook's rounding. \textbf{HTML} does not: the released
driver splits features and labels with three independent unseeded shuffles, and with
the authors' model classes verbatim on rebuilt inputs the published recipe (ten
epochs, learning rate $2{\times}10^{-5}$, raw log-volatility targets) is still
converging at epoch ten; it beats the paper only at $\tau{=}3$ on a random split with
dropout 0, never reaches persistence at $\tau\ge15$ on any split, and on the VolTAGE
split every later paper inherited it is worse than persistence at every horizon. Its
text$+$audio row reproduces under the shipped code's random split and under no
other. \textbf{Sawhney et al.} cannot run as released (an unshipped call inventory,
price files and lexicons, a TensorFlow~2.1 stack); ported, the finance SVR alone gives
0.712/0.370/0.218/0.154, the text and audio branches are untrained after the two and
one epochs the scripts prescribe, and the published ensemble is reproduced because
the released code tunes the mixing weights on the test set, where an untrained
branch's near-constant output acts as an intercept. \textbf{DialogueGAT}'s corpus
and labels were never released; ported onto FinCall's speaker turns under the
paper's per-year protocol it beats persistence at short horizons and collapses on
the 2020 split. \textbf{KeFVP}'s released script needed six import-level repairs
before it would start; its MAEC rows use sentence embeddings regenerated with the
authors' own generator because the released ones were never published, and seven of
eight cells fall within one published standard deviation (the eighth, MAEC-16 at
$\tau{=}15$, is the row of their table that does not average to its own stated
mean). On EC the released KePt embeddings are unavailable upstream (the linked
folder is empty); with BERT-large pooler embeddings from the same generator, KeFVP
without its knowledge-enhanced pre-training, the mean test MSE is
0.651/0.302/0.194/0.127 against the published 0.610/0.291/0.183/0.114, within one of
our repeat standard deviations but above all four published means, so the row is
not counted as reproduced.

Under the controls, the 30-session embargo removes half to two thirds of KeFVP's
MAEC training calls, so those rows cannot separate leakage from data loss and are
left to the ledger; DialogueGAT's port (one seed, 42--60 held-out calls per year)
shows no consistent direction under either control.

\subsection{Post-cutoff evaluation under the call-date anchor}
Table~\ref{tab:lookahead-assumed} repeats the post-cutoff evaluation with day~0
anchored on the call date on both sides of the cutoff, the anchor every released
label set uses. HAR-RV barely moves, but the ridge heads that carry a learned
past-volatility block collapse in-cutoff under that anchor, consistent with the block
absorbing the reaction session that the after-hours rule leaves inside the
pre-window for before-open calls. On $\Delta v$ at $\tau{=}30$ the frozen WavLM$+$vol
head is the only one whose post-cutoff CI excludes zero under the measured anchor
(0.18, CI 0.14--0.24, against HAR's 0.08), a single-quarter observation without a
DM test that we report as exploratory.
