\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence shows.}
On the completed ladder (Stages 0--4), four statements hold. (i)~Strong econometric
baselines are hard to beat, and the headline metric (\Rtwo{} vs.\ persistence)
exposes a long-horizon regime failure (FinCall temporal $\tau{=}30$) that raw MSE
would obscure. (ii)~Frozen text content does not cleanly add signal over
past-volatility and identity baselines on the identity-robust \dv{} target, and the
reason is measurable: call embeddings encode ticker identity (89.5\% probe accuracy
on FinCall), and within-ticker transcript shuffles leave performance essentially
unchanged---an in-corpus replication of the ``Same Company, Same Signal'' effect
\citep{scss2024}. (iii)~Frozen audio content is inert: audio-only heads sit at
\Rtwo{} zero everywhere, the apparent gains of past-vol-augmented audio heads
survive a \emph{global} audio shuffle, and WavLM embeddings still encode the
speaker's company at 76.3\% probe accuracy---identity without predictive content.
(iv)~Fusion does not rescue the modalities it fuses: multimodal never significantly
beats the best unimodal model (RQ2 negative). Together these form one coherent
finding: \emph{in this task family, what looks like content signal is
overwhelmingly company identity plus past volatility.}

\paragraph{Why this is a benchmark paper.}
Every one of those statements depends on apparatus the shared substrate does not
have. The identity probe and the shuffle controls need a ticker per call, which
FinCall-Surprise does not ship; the \tickerdisjoint{} rows need a split the lineage
never reports; the regime finding needs per-year breakdowns over recomputed
targets rather than inherited label files; and the embargo makes the temporal
numbers honest at all (\S\ref{sec:substrate}). A field that evaluates on files with
a zero-day embargo, majority company overlap, and an unstated horizon convention
cannot distinguish a model from a memory of which company it is looking at.
\ecvol-bench is the repair, and the negative results above are what the repaired
substrate reports for the modern representations we could run. The reproduction
study (\S\ref{sec:results-repro}) is the same test applied to the field's own
models on the field's own data; it is the part of the argument this draft still
owes.

\paragraph{Engaging the strongest positive claims.}
The strongest recent positive claim---up to 43.8\% of 30-day realised-volatility
variance from text$+$acoustic features \citep{soundofrisk2025}---reports no
identity controls, no released code, and a proprietary corpus. Prosody was the
modality most plausibly carrying content orthogonal to identity; our modern audio
ladder (WavLM, emotion2vec+, eGeMAPS) finds it inert once past volatility is
controlled---albeit on the corpus's 40\,kbps source audio (see Limitations and
\S\ref{sec:results-clean-audio})---and our shuffle controls predict such gains
would not survive a within-ticker (let alone global) shuffle, a check the released
suite makes cheap. Likewise, ECC Analyzer \citep{cao2024ecc} reports large MSE
reductions from LLM-extracted semantics with closed-API components and no released
code; it cannot be reproduced, and we say so in the code-availability audit rather
than argue with it.

\paragraph{The pre-registered framing gate.}
The study was pre-registered with a framing gate: a ``positive showcase'' spine was
to be adopted only if content-bearing models beat the Stage-0/1 floor on \dv{} or
HAR-residual with DM-significant improvements that survived the identity-control
suite on at least two of four horizons. The criterion was tested and missed at
Stage~2 (text), Stage~3 (audio), and Stage~4 (fusion), and the gate was recorded as
resolved toward re-examination in the project's decision log before the benchmark
framing was adopted. We report this so that the framing of this paper can be
audited against what the data said and when.

\paragraph{What a clean answer would need.}
Independent of any model, two facts about the corpora limit every result in this
literature and are addressed only by new data: the uniform 40\,kbps transcode
underlying all FinCall-Surprise acoustics, and the absence of any measured call
time of day. The first is why the Earnings25 clean-audio re-run
(\S\ref{sec:results-clean-audio}) is the single most informative pending
experiment---it either makes the audio-inert finding near-unassailable or
overturns it; the second is why the timestamp retrofit (\S\ref{sec:timing}) is
part of the benchmark rather than a footnote.
