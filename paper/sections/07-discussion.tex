\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence shows.}
On the completed ladder (Stages 0--5), five statements hold. (i)~Strong econometric
baselines are hard to beat, and the headline metric (\Rtwo{} vs.\ persistence)
exposes a long-horizon regime failure (FinCall temporal $\tau{=}30$) that raw MSE
would obscure. (ii)~Frozen text content does not cleanly add signal over
past-volatility and identity baselines on the identity-robust \dv{} target, and the
reason is measurable: call embeddings encode ticker identity (89.5\% probe accuracy
on FinCall), and within-ticker transcript shuffles leave performance essentially
unchanged---an in-corpus replication of the ``Same Company, Same Signal'' effect
\citep{scss2024}. (iii)~Frozen audio content is inert: audio-only heads sit at
\Rtwo{} zero everywhere, the apparent gains of past-vol-augmented audio heads
survive a \emph{global} audio shuffle, and WavLM embeddings still encode the
speaker's company at 76.3\% probe accuracy---identity without predictive content.
(iv)~Fusion does not rescue the modalities it fuses: multimodal never significantly
beats the best unimodal model (RQ2 negative). (v)~LLM-extracted ``auditable''
semantic features fail their own audit: constrained decoding delivers 100\%
schema-valid output whose content agrees with a human rater at $\kappa\le0.222$ on
every confirmatory field, far below the pre-registered 0.6 gate, with capability
levers (prompt anchors, quantization, 2$\times$ scale) all measurably null.
Together these form one coherent finding: \emph{in this task family, what looks
like content signal is overwhelmingly company identity plus past volatility, and
the semantic features the recent literature consumes are not validated
measurements.}

\paragraph{Engaging the strongest positive claims.}
The strongest recent positive claim---up to 43.8\% of 30-day realised-volatility
variance from text$+$acoustic features \citep{soundofrisk2025}---reports no
identity controls. Prosody was the modality most plausibly carrying content
orthogonal to identity; our modern audio ladder (WavLM, emotion2vec+, eGeMAPS)
finds it inert once past volatility is controlled---albeit on the corpus's
40\,kbps source audio (see Limitations)---and our shuffle controls predict
such gains would not survive a within-ticker (let alone global) shuffle---a check
we encourage and that our released suite makes cheap. Likewise, ECC Analyzer
\citep{cao2024ecc} reports large MSE reductions from LLM-extracted semantics with
no human validation of extraction quality; our Stage-5 gate is exactly that
validation, and it failed for every confirmatory field.

\paragraph{The framing gate (pre-registered; provisionally resolved).}
The paper's spine is decided at a pre-registered gate, not retro-fitted. \emph{Path
A (positive showcase)} is adopted only if content-bearing models beat the Stage-0/1
floor on \dv{} or HAR-residual with DM-significant improvements that survive the
identity-control suite on at least two of four horizons. \emph{Path B (rigorous
re-examination)} is adopted otherwise. The Path-A criterion has now been tested and
missed at Stage~2 (text), Stage~3 (audio), and Stage~4 (fusion), and Stage~5 was
blocked at validity; the gate is therefore \emph{provisionally resolved to Path~B}
(recorded in the project decision log), with final confirmation deferred to the
post-cutoff lookahead test (Phase~7). This draft remains framing-neutral in tone
pending that last experiment.

\paragraph{Instrument, not model.}
The Stage-5 failure decomposes informatively (\S\ref{sec:results-llm}). Part is
instrument defect: the rubric's ``hedging recurs'' anchor is trivially true of any
25k-character Q\&A section, so the model's constant ``moderate'' is arguably
rubric-compliant while the human applied an unstated relative baseline---an
ambiguity no model scale fixes, and consistent with the one non-null control
(rater-baseline exemplars helping exactly that field). Part is genuine
non-discrimination (near-constant analyst-tone output). And part is structural to
the gate itself: $\kappa>0.6$ against a single unvalidated rater has an unmeasured
human--human ceiling, and subjective ordinal annotation routinely ceilings at
0.4--0.6. The constructive programme is therefore behaviourally anchored, coarser
rubrics plus a measured annotator ceiling---not larger models.

\paragraph{Why the contribution holds either way.}
Independent of the gate, \ecvol{} contributes an open, identity-controlled,
leakage-proof multimodal benchmark with honest baselines and a reusable diagnostic
suite---identity probes, shuffle controls, and an extraction-validity gate---
precisely the apparatus the field has lacked. A negative or mixed answer to
RQ1--RQ4 is a publishable result that the existing literature does not provide.
Beyond the lookahead test, the natural next step is data rather than models: a
forward-collected corpus with native identity and exact call timestamps (a capture
pilot is underway) removes the reconstruction and after-hours-assumption caveats
that this generation of corpora imposes.
